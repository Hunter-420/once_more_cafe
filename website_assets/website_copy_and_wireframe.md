## 1. Brand Identity & Creative Direction

### Brand Essence
**Once More - Cafe & Store** is an intimate sanctuary where thoughtful hospitality meets artisanal craft. Nestled in Eden Terrace, Auckland, it balances the precision of specialty espresso, the mindful ritual of single-estate New Zealand and Japanese teas, freshly baked cabinet delicacies, and curated European homeware.

### Brand Keywords
`Warmth` · `Mindful Pace` · `Artisanal Simplicity` · `Ceramic Aesthetics` · `Quiet Sanctuary` · `Community Connection`

### Recommended Design Palette
- **Alabaster Warm White (Background)**: `#FCFAF6`
- **Espresso Roast (Primary Text & Headings)**: `#27221E`
- **Warm Terracotta (Accent 1)**: `#BC654B`
- **Matcha Sage Olive (Accent 2)**: `#5F7359`
- **Soft Oat Cream (Card & Container BG)**: `#F6F2EB`
- **Antique Gold (Ratings & Badges)**: `#C99632`
- **Muted Stone (Borders & Dividers)**: `#E8E2D8`

### Typography Hierarchy
- **Display / Hero**: Editorial Serif (*Cormorant Garamond*, *Playfair Display*, or *Fraunces*)
- **Body & Interface**: Modern Accessible Sans-Serif (*Plus Jakarta Sans* or *Inter*)
- **Accents / Subtitles**: Elegant Monospace or Small Caps with relaxed letter-spacing

## 4. Technical SEO & Responsive Engineering

1. **Schema.org Structured Data**:
   - Implements `@graph` schema combining `CafeOrCoffeeShop`, `Store`, `Menu`, and `AggregateRating`.
   - Links directly to local Google Knowledge Graph entity `/g/11rv17b51m` and Google Maps Place ID `ChIJDxiuQlhHDW0RI8mLwDLveNA`.
2. **Local Geotagging**:
   - `geo.region`: `NZ-AUK`
   - `geo.placename`: `Eden Terrace, Auckland`
   - `geo.position`: `-36.863458;174.7613921`
   - `ICBM`: `-36.863458, 174.7613921`
3. **Responsive Architecture**:
   - Zero emoji dependency: 100% vector SVG icons for retina crispness across all platforms.
   - Fluid typography with CSS `clamp()`.
   - Accessible mobile navigation drawer with keyboard `Esc` closing and aria states.
   - Zero horizontal overflow on devices from 320px wide up to 4K displays.
